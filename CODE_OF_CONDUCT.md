# Code of Conduct

Adopted from Contributor Covenant v2.1. For users and contributors — not AI.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone.

## Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior: open an issue or email the maintainer. All complaints reviewed promptly and fairly.
